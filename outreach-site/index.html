GOOD QUESTIONS, STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Before you begin.

If something matters to your decision, we want you to know it up front.

Can I start using Unravler Outreach today?

Access is currently invite-only. We review each pilot request, confirm the required sender region and billing, then help set up eligible accounts. There is no instant checkout or public trial.

Do I need to give you my LinkedIn password?

No. You sign in on LinkedIn yourself and, if you choose to connect a sender, manually provide session values. These are sensitive credentials and are encrypted at rest. The outreach connection is separate from official OAuth features in Unravler's scheduler.

Is Unravler affiliated with or approved by LinkedIn?

No. Unravler is not affiliated with LinkedIn. Session-based outreach uses unofficial interfaces; automated actions may violate LinkedIn's terms and carry account restriction or suspension risk.

What does a sender seat include?

One paid seat is intended for one connected LinkedIn sender, with a dedicated proxy lease during its active provider term. Pilot availability depends on a suitable region-matched IP and an operator-approved subscription.

I already use Unravler's social scheduler. Is this the same subscription?

No. Outreach is a separate product and subscription. Existing scheduler customers can ask about a pilot bundle quote; eligibility and any discount are confirmed before invoicing.
