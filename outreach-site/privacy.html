Privacy Policy

This policy explains how the Unravler Outreach pilot handles information about workspace members, connected senders, and prospects.

Who operates the service

UNRAVLER TECHNOLOGIES (Udyam registration UDYAM-JH-20-0144275) operates this service from Om Niwas, Near Over Bridge, Anantpur Road, New Anantpur, Ranchi, Jharkhand 834002, India. Contact us at contact@unravler.com.

This page applies to the standalone outreach marketing site and outreach pilot. The existing Unravler social publishing and scheduling product has its own policies. Its official LinkedIn OAuth connection is separate from the outreach session connection described here.

Information we handle

  • Account and workspace information: name, email address, workspace membership, roles, support messages, and account activity.
  • Sender connection information: LinkedIn session values that a member deliberately provides, including li_at and JSESSIONID, optional li_a, selected sender region, connection status, and related profile details. We do not ask for a LinkedIn password in this flow.
  • Outreach information: leads, profile and post information, campaign settings, drafts, messages, inbox records, and recorded engagement events created or retrieved for the workspace.
  • Billing and operations information: access requests, sender-seat counts, requested country, invoice and payment-confirmation records, paid-through date, and cancellation requests. The pilot does not have a public card checkout.
  • Technical information: IP address, browser and device details, logs, diagnostics, and security events generated when the site or service is used.

Why we use it

We use this information to operate workspaces, verify and manage sender connections, display prospect and campaign records, carry out features you choose to use, respond to support and privacy requests, investigate abuse or failures, and administer the manually invoiced pilot.

If a workspace member requests AI-generated text, relevant post and prompt content may be processed by an AI service provider to return a draft. The draft should be reviewed by a person before publication. We do not use the outreach session connection as a substitute for official LinkedIn OAuth permissions.

Session connections and provider proxies

The outreach connection uses session values rather than a LinkedIn password. The current sender-connection flow encrypts those values before database storage. When a sender action is enabled and initiated, requests may be routed through a country-matched, sender-assigned proxy supplied by a third-party provider. That provider may process network metadata necessary to deliver the connection.

The paid pilot is invite-only and live LinkedIn actions are not enabled by default. LinkedIn's User Agreement restricts unauthorized automated methods. A proxy or a usage limit does not make such activity compliant or remove account-restriction risk.

Who may receive information

We use service providers for hosting, storage, authentication, email, proxy connectivity, operational support, and optional AI features. Providers receive information only as needed for the service they perform. Information may also be disclosed where required by law or to address security or abuse. The pilot is invoiced manually; this site does not collect payment-card numbers.

Prospect information and messages are visible to authorized members of your workspace according to their access. Your use of prospect data should respect applicable privacy, marketing, and platform rules.

Retention, security, and your choices

We retain information while needed to provide the service and for legitimate operational, security, accounting, and legal purposes. When a paid outreach period ends, active work is paused, queued actions are canceled, and sender accounts are marked for reauthorization. Campaign, lead, and invoice records are not automatically erased merely because a subscription ends.

We use access controls and encryption for current sender connection secrets, but no internet service can promise absolute security. You can ask to access, correct, or delete information associated with your workspace by following our data deletion instructions or emailing contact@unravler.com. We will assess the request, verify authority, and explain any information we must retain.

Marketing-site cookies and changes

This standalone public marketing site does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. See our Cookie Notice for details. An authenticated application may need account-related browser storage; this policy will be updated before a public application launch if that changes the practices described here.

We may update this policy as the pilot evolves. The date at the top will show the latest version. For privacy questions, use the contact page.
