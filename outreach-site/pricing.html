BEFORE YOU CONNECT

Know the limits as well as the features.

Unravler Outreach's session connection uses manually supplied LinkedIn session tokens and unofficial interfaces. Automated activity may violate LinkedIn's rules and may lead to account restriction or suspension. A residential or ISP proxy does not make that activity compliant or risk-free.

We do not ask for your LinkedIn password. Sender connection and any live-action enablement are separate operator-controlled steps during the pilot.

PRICING QUESTIONS

The details, up front.

Is the $59 price per person or per LinkedIn sender?

Per connected sender seat. A workspace can request 1–5 seats in the current pilot. Each connected sender requires its own eligible dedicated proxy lease.

Can I pay in the app?

Not yet. The pilot uses a verified external invoice and operator approval. We do not activate access based only on a request, and this site does not collect payment.

Can I choose a different country later?

Tell us the required country before onboarding. The current pilot supports one requested country per workspace; mixed-country teams need a separate review and cannot be silently assigned an unrelated IP.

What happens when a paid period ends?

Paid outreach access stops and active work is paused. Connected sender session values are removed from the active account record. A paid proxy lease can remain assigned until its already-paid provider term ends; we do not promise immediate release or an unchanged IP on a future subscription.

Is there a refund or free trial?

There is no free trial. Review the billing and cancellations page and your invoice terms before payment. We confirm any exception in writing rather than implying an automatic refund policy.
