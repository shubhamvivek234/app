Cookie Notice

A short, specific explanation of browser storage on this standalone marketing site.

What this site uses today

The public marketing pages currently do not set advertising or analytics cookies and do not load third-party ad trackers. The navigation and page interactions run in the browser without saving a persistent preference. Our web host may still process ordinary request logs, including IP address and browser information, to deliver and protect the site.

The outreach application is different

An authenticated application may require session-related cookies or browser storage to keep a member signed in and protect an account. LinkedIn sender session values that a user submits are separate sensitive credentials used for the outreach connection; they are not a marketing-site tracking cookie. Our Privacy Policy explains that connection in more detail.

Before we add optional analytics, advertising, or a public sign-in flow to this site, we will review and update this notice and any necessary consent controls.

Your controls

You can delete or block cookies and site data through your browser settings. Blocking essential authentication storage in a future application could prevent sign-in from working. If you have a question about this notice, email contact@unravler.com.
