MULTI-SENDER FAQS

Frequently asked questions.

How many sender seats can I request in the pilot?

Workspaces can request between 1 and 5 sender seats in the current pilot phase, subject to proxy availability.

Can team members operate in different countries?

The current pilot supports one requested country per workspace to maintain rigorous IP verification. Mixed-country teams require separate operator review.

What happens if a team member leaves?

You can disconnect the sender seat and reconnect a new representative. The dedicated proxy lease remains active until provider renewal.
