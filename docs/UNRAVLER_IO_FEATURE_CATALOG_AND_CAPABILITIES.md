# Unravler.io — Feature Catalog and Capabilities
Version: 2.22.0 | Updated: 2026-10-05 | Status: Corrected capability baseline and target contracts

## Evidence contract
Feature availability, local implementation, automated verification and live provider verification are separate fields.
Baseline local commit: 2187946. Architecture-review validation: 355 outreach backend tests passed. Those tests do not establish live provider behavior.
Each release adds code/test references and names remaining targets. A mixed “verified/planned” heading is not an acceptance criterion.

## Product and access boundaries
Unravler.io is the outreach product. The wider Unravler scheduler and Smart Bio have separate availability and integration permissions.
Customer-supplied files, staged review, AI drafts and manual Engage review are preparation workflows. Authenticated search crawling and LinkedIn inbox/profile reads are private-endpoint automation and remain gated alongside mutations.
Do not claim residential proxies, jitter, or manual initiation makes authenticated crawling risk-free. [LinkedIn policy](https://www.linkedin.com/help/linkedin/answer/a1341387/prohibited-software-and-extensions).

## 4. AI personalization
Local: draft generation and prompt/template tools. The sequence executor rejects preview-only AI prompt tokens rather than generating them during live dispatch.
Target: a separately verified prompt-injection/sanitization contract for untrusted profile/post text; deterministic length and link/mention policy per action and account tier.
AI output never grants billing, procurement, sender access, content approval or execution permission.
No automatic graduation after 50 messages. Autonomous operation requires an explicit authorized campaign policy decision.

## 5. Content approval and outcome review
Local: tenant-scoped review of uncertain actions, with actor, timestamp and human evidence note. Outcome review does not send, automatically resume, or prove remote delivery.
Local: Content reviews in Settings binds exact rendered copy to workspace, sender/generation, recipient, enrollment, sequence version/digest, node, campaign revision and policy version/digest. Identified decisions use revision fences and 24-hour expiry. Editing or regeneration invalidates the review; approval does not grant execution or resume a campaign. Voice review binds cached encrypted audio bytes/digest. Email checks the approved mailbox identity and effective MIME copy at its final provider boundary; Gmail requires a nonempty accepted message ID and Graph permits its documented 202 response without an ID. Acceptance does not prove delivery.
The campaign launch gate already rejects uncertain task records; it does not replace worker-side content approval enforcement.
Resolution may remain unknown, record provider/read-back evidence, record human assertion or stop a lead. Do not force every outcome into confirmed delivery or confirmed failure.
Evidence: outreach/api/action_reconciliation.py, outreach/api/campaigns.py, tests/test_outreach_action_reconciliation.py.

## Offline campaign voice preparation
Local tenth slice: explicit Prepare persists a policy/context-bound job; media workers make one bounded paid TTS call after a durable generating marker and daily workspace budget reservation. Final owner/context checks commit encrypted PCM WAV, digest/generation identity, pending review and receipt together. HTTP stays outside transaction retries. Missing receipts remain held without replay; saved preparation receipts reuse exact bytes. Review and sequence execution never generate audio.
The policy permits 1,000 finished script characters, 60 seconds of mono 16-bit/16 kHz PCM, and 20 clips / 20,000 characters per workspace per UTC day. Recordings expire after 24 hours; new generation identity requires fresh review even for identical bytes. Current artifact ownership and exact captured approval are rechecked before transport, including recipient/context changes with prior approvals for both choices. Erasure scrubs script/audio and retains minimal unknown generation evidence without TTL; stopped-work recovery changes only bounded scheduling metadata and cannot reopen the workspace.
Generation has a separate disabled-by-default OUTREACH_VOICE_PREPARATION_ENABLED gate; live sending remains independently disabled. At the tenth-slice boundary, legacy Voice Studio cloning, sample previews and vendor deletion remained synchronous; the seventeenth slice below supersedes that bounded lifecycle/consent/content-custody gap. Media queues do not prove process/network/credential isolation. No live TTS/model/format or LinkedIn uploader compatibility was verified. ## Local managed proxy custody and orphan storage repair
Managed IPRoyal password custody is locally implemented in the opt-in `kms_v1` mode. Its authenticated context binds workspace, canonical asset ID, credential generation, transport metadata, purpose and environment. Sender ownership remains a separate one-sender lease and final authorization check. Imported inventory and embedded sender copies retain custody metadata; KMS mode refuses unscoped legacy assets. At the twelfth-slice boundary, mailbox/finder/voice/shared-secret custody, Webshare provisioning and actual workload IAM remained separate; the thirteenth slice below supersedes the mailbox/finder scope.
Each transport opens the proxy before authorization and captures the exact encrypted asset plus actual URL from the same snapshot. Mongo authorization compares that prepared binding against the current sender and inventory without KMS calls; replacements, workspace swaps and stale generations deny transport. Drained migration updates a managed asset and its proven sender aliases with an atomic metadata receipt, preserving leases, installed cookie generation and stopped permission. A saved receipt retries without key calls or automatic resume.
New connection locks retain a reference-only base-account/resource proof before attempting pending insertion. A private, all-writer-drained, live-disabled stop can close an absent job only when that proof still matches and no verification/installation evidence exists. It atomically stops permission, saves a permanent reference-only closure and removes only job-owned unused reservations/lock. Existing reconnect credentials and reused leases/slots remain held; older ambiguous locks, changed accounts, physical evidence or a now-present job require separate review. The closure omits TTL-indexed `created_at`; late insertion cannot reopen it. Workspace erasure can purge ordinary closure metadata only behind its permanent workspace exclusion. The connection dialog recognizes stopped terminal states. Procedure and evidence: [Proxy custody and storage repair](UNRAVLER_IO_PROXY_CUSTODY_AND_STORAGE_REPAIR_RELEASE.md).

## Local LinkedIn credential custody
Opt-in OUTREACH_CREDENTIAL_CUSTODY_MODE=kms_v1 binds pending/installed cookie envelopes to independently supplied workspace, sender, generation, purpose and environment. Async consumers reject plaintext/Fernet/mock/context swaps in KMS mode. No key-service call occurs inside Mongo retries. A configured exact temporary role and key ARN are checked; real AWS policies and container isolation remain unverified.
Private stopped/drained migration accepts strictly decryptable legacy Fernet rows, preserves generation/latched permission, invalidates inbox coverage and atomically saves a receipt. Pending storage rechecks paid/lock/erasure authorities; ambiguous acknowledgements retain job/resources. Legacy remains the default until cutover. Environment binding is not restore execution-epoch fencing. The twelfth slice below extends custody to managed IPRoyal passwords; the thirteenth extends mailbox/finder custody. Voice/shared secrets and Webshare provisioning remain separate work. Contract and operator procedure: [LinkedIn credential custody](UNRAVLER_IO_CREDENTIAL_CUSTODY_RELEASE.md).
