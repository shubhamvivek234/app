## Implemented behavior

New supported original invitations retain an `outreach_connection_waits` record. Its identity binds the native lead/campaign/sender, immutable sequence/version/digest, enrollment, authorization revision, credential generation, original identity-field digest, original invite intent/attempt and actual submitted person URN. The wait starts at the original durable attempt confirmation, rather than retry time. Exact original received invitation metadata, logical operation, transactional authority references and BSON-preserving native hashes are required again when using the scope. ObjectId-to-same-text-string replacement cannot adopt the original invite. Every new connection-request attempt stamps the modern contract before transport, including when its result metadata is missing. Unsupported success-response schemas never fabricate confirmation. Modern broken proofs hold; genuinely older attempts without any modern target/request/contract metadata retain ordinary waits and gain no F6 authority.

The existing due sequence job rechecks only the original immutable recipient while on the invite wait or a due step exclusive to its negative branch. It never resolves a current vanity URL for this observation. Positive-path shared joins and finished/stopped leads are outside the negative wait; no continuous event monitor, immediate polling during delays or reopening of terminal work is implemented. Before-deadline recorded negatives wait until another due check. Unknown at the deadline or on the negative path holds. A supported recorded negative at the deadline exits through the original negative target and saved delay.

A fresh recorded positive redirects to the original positive target with its saved delay, or safely finishes if that target is absent. One guarded snapshot/majority transaction cancels only exact proven unsubmitted original negative intents/tasks/jobs, increments the scope/lead revision and saves the first redirect receipt. Repeated delivery retains that receipt and due time without entering the positive path twice. Accepted, possible, unknown, malformed, foreign or ambiguous current work remains retained and holds for review. Proven completed LinkedIn visit/like predecessors can remain untouched; completed communication or paid finder predecessors require review in this slice. No send is undone, replayed, renewed or classified as unsubmitted by expiry.

## Ordering and physical evidence

Observation-fact publication, redirect, scoped condition/step advancement and physical final authorization share the existing workspace suppression guard. Current campaign/sender/permission/payment/resource and original evidence rows are read and fenced in the transaction. Reply/DNC/erasure/epoch and current source stops outrank a redirect. A competing positive cannot be bypassed by an email-availability condition advancing into a shared join. Nested redirect failure propagates to the owning transaction, so cancellation/scope writes cannot partially commit before a failed final lead CAS.

Each due observation uses the existing RequestEnvelope, source permission, sender token, custody/proxy checks and physical budgets, including when the due negative step is email. LinkedIn inbox freshness and reply suppression are rechecked for that source. Final negative-action authorization rejoins its exact received false fact, original request/context/operation and timestamp, rather than trusting a lead/scope projection. Its 60-second deadline is rechecked synchronously after commit before HTTP. If acceptance commits first, final negative HTTP is denied; if negative authorization commits first, original possible/received evidence remains and acceptance cannot silently cancel it.

The supported parser is deliberately narrow: typed person URNs, bounded known record/paging shapes, exact integers, optional public identifiers as scalar text of at most 200 characters, and no error/unknown nested status fields. Exact membership can be recorded from a partial supported page; absence requires its recorded complete-page shape. The received-body parser checks 1 MB and 1,000 rows; this does not provide a streaming socket/body-memory bound. Recorded page completeness, HTTP status and local authority references do not establish independently authenticated provider completeness, provider acceptance time, invitation causality or official API support. No live LinkedIn/source fidelity test is claimed.

## Recovery and recipient limits

Saved positives are checked before a new charged GET and before negative authorization/advancement. The durable context binds scope, revision, native lead, original eligible node and stable sequence job, so worker-owner replacement and an original wait's competing deadline exit cannot erase the saved positive with a later negative. All saved facts remain reference-bound; malformed/overflow evidence holds. Negative cancellation scans are capped at 200 records per queried collection, with a separate cap of 200 entries for each retained attempt/non-call history. These are query/validation bounds, not an arbitrary database-byte or authenticated completeness claim.

Positive observations older than **60 seconds require review**. Production sequence job/lead leases are **120 seconds**; therefore ordinary redelivery after a full lease expiry does not automatically redirect from that stale observation, even with renewed source permission. A later negative cannot overwrite it. The local crash drills accelerate Redis visibility to three seconds and force only disposable fixture leases expired; they prove prompt-recovery mechanics and non-replay, not automatic recovery under production timing. A separate simulated 121-second regression proves the default-duration hold. No automatic stale-positive release/operator repair or dedicated hold-review UI is added here.

F6 does not implement generic recipient resolution. A scoped URN-capable LinkedIn step must still use a saved lead transport URN matching the original invited member and satisfy existing content review. URL-only originals cannot silently acquire a new transport identity. Profile visits and legacy IF_CONNECTED transports hold because their current adapters cannot faithfully prove that immutable recipient. Separately reviewed rebind/migration and fuller F4 transport contracts remain pending. Email/finder retain their existing recipient/content authorities and commercial uncertainty rules.

## Storage, deletion and rollout

Required unique workspace/original-invite-intent/attempt and scope/lead/job/observation lookup indexes are added locally, without TTL, adoption or duplicate cleanup. Startup preserves the existing retention-first index order; conflicts fail rather than discarding data. Wait scopes contain personal recipient data and join the guarded erasure inventory. Actual erasure batches delete them and remove new recipient/fact/context metadata from retained minimal physical/intent holds; foreign workspace records remain. The observation collection is the existing physical request ledger, not a competing queue or feed.

The scheduler no longer initializes a missing current node by falling back to root and bypassing its saved delay. Existing launch/intake own initialization. Superseded jobs cannot be rewritten completed/deferred by a returning old worker. General immutable activity coverage, legacy adoption, authenticated restore/reconciliation/activation, security/npm/container/protected checks, live health/IAM/egress/pilot and combined production release remain open. Live/conditional defaults are unchanged; no commit, push, deployment, production migration/grant, purchase or provider/AWS call occurs in this slice.
