# Unravler.io — Content approval and manual-action recovery

Date: 2026-10-02. Local implementation only; no commit, push, deployment, provider calls, purchases or execution grants. Live-action and conditional-launch flags retain their disabled defaults.

## Implemented behavior

Campaign invitation notes, messages and voice notes require an identified human's approval of the rendered content. Approval binds workspace, sender, credential generation, source, recipient, enrollment, node, immutable sequence version/digest, campaign authorization revision and content-policy version/digest. A content or context change produces a different review. Decisions use revision-fenced updates and expire after 24 hours; worker creation of a pending review cannot overwrite a rejection or revocation.

Settings → Content reviews displays the exact recipient, sender and copy. Administrators/owners can prepare assigned current steps without provider calls, approve after an explicit review acknowledgement, reject, revoke, and inspect history. Preparation and review listing are paginated. Preparation does not assign leads, launch/resume campaigns, grant sender permission, or enable live actions. Launch/resume changes campaign revision: prepare and review its current assigned steps after that revision change. Draft campaigns without assigned steps have nothing executable to review.

LinkedIn adapters compare their captured recipient and content before transport. The physical guard checks approval and performs a final approval-document CAS after the sender/permission CAS, with explicit expiry checks before the lazy transport call. Manual likes and the like stage of a reviewed comment also use this approval boundary. Sequence post likes without text retain their existing distinct policy.

These are two ordering boundaries, not a cross-document transaction. Revocation of content before the approval CAS blocks the call; revocation after authorization does not cancel a remote request. A sender/campaign/paid/asset change between different document checks remains a strict-ordering release blocker. This release does not claim global atomic cancellation.

## Prepared voice clips

One prepared clip is stored encrypted, with a SHA-256 digest, bounded byte size, preparation ownership and a 24-hour expiry. A reviewed action sends those cached bytes rather than synthesizing another clip. Changed voice identity, script, context or audio requires another review. The preview endpoint validates tenant and expected audio digest, returns private/no-store media, and cannot substitute regenerated bytes under an earlier review.

The UI enables the acknowledgement after playback reaches the end; this is a human assertion, not proof that a person listened. The API records explicit review acknowledgement. Preview preparation reads already-cached clips and does not call a voice vendor. A worker prepares uncached clips during the guarded execution flow; a separate offline preparation workflow and live vendor verification remain future work. Preparation ownership is local: it is not a vendor idempotency guarantee. The expiry index bounds cached clip custody; verify it exists during rollout.

## Durable manual Engage and inbox actions

An explicit Send, Comment or Like click stores the identified actor, exact choice and immutable binding in MongoDB. Brokers receive references only, without reply/comment bodies, credentials or audio. Queue-publication failures retain the saved job. Workers load that record, validate the target and sender again, and cannot treat an old plaintext broker payload as approval.

Manual jobs keep separate pending, possible-submission and provider-accepted stages. For “like then comment,” a received like is not repeated when the comment has a proven non-call deferral. A crash after possible submission does not cause automatic resend. Receipt recovery must match the exact physical request, owner, workspace/sender, generation, source, logical operation and action. Stable same-post physical deduplication remains in force across jobs.

Received outcomes repair reporting without sending again. Engage counters and inbox messages are conditionally projected once. Inbox replies share the sync producer's `thread_revision`, so a newly committed inbound message invalidates a queued reply and an older sync cannot overwrite a projected reply. A newer inbound snippet/timestamp is preserved. Per-thread atomic reply slots and a partial unique active-target index block concurrent jobs and uncertain-action replacement.

Completion and cancellation cleanup are idempotent. Recovery can clear only a proven receipt's matching transport token and release only its original owner with an atomic token-absent CAS. Cleanup never clears another job's post/thread slot or a newer sender owner. Provider restrictions still pause the sender through SafetyShield.

Administrators can renew a still-current manual approval or cancel a queued, proven-unattempted remainder. Completed stages stay in history. Active or uncertain actions cannot be cancelled to create another send. Unknown manual outcomes remain held for drained operator reconciliation; a complete human-resolution/read-back tool for these jobs is not implemented here.

Due publication and receipt/projection recovery run every 30 seconds. Publication throttling is separate from execution due time. Ordered due timestamps and recovery backoff prevent repeatedly deferred/unknown rows from starving later work. These tests simulate broker failure; they do not establish real broker/process-restart behavior.

Correction to the preceding report: Engage already used background workers before this slice. This change replaces plaintext worker payloads and unsafe lock cleanup with durable approved jobs; it does not introduce background Engage transport for the first time.

## Email boundary

Email sequence preflight now reviews normalized recipient, stripped subject, sending mailbox identity and the effective body including the existing STOP footer. The preview and MIME builder share body rendering; mailbox reconnection changes the review identity. An unapproved email does not consume the legacy daily allowance during initial preflight.

Email/finder providers still use their existing transport. They do not yet share the LinkedIn physical envelope or its final content/permission CAS. Do not claim universal final-payload authorization, physical budgets, or complete known-non-call recovery for email. This remains the next transport cutover, including refreshed-token/mailbox-generation checks at the provider boundary.

## Cutover and next work

1. Keep live flags disabled. Back up state and drain old workers before any deployment of this protocol. Mixed live versions are unsupported.
2. Verify approval/manual due indexes, the active-target uniqueness index and the voice expiry index. Atomic thread/post claims remain necessary even with indexes. Check the production MongoDB version supports the partial index expression.
3. Inventory old queued Engage/reply payloads and locks. These workers reject references without a durable approved job. Do not silently upgrade plaintext broker content into authorization; use reviewed, drained cleanup and a fresh explicit human choice where non-submission is proven.
4. Exercise approval/revocation, queue outage, receipt repair, cancellation and unknown-outcome holds on isolated fixtures. Verify saved content erasure/retention: existing account erasure still omits outreach collections and is a release blocker.
5. Next implement the separate sequence-intent publisher, final email/finder provider boundaries and real broker/process-crash drills. Then finish manual unknown-outcome and connection-finalization repair tooling/custody bounds.
6. Verify strict authority ordering, credential custody, proxy-only routing, restore fencing, purchased IPRoyal seats and the mailbox pilot before a separate live-action release decision. Manual verified-invoice provisioning remains the pilot authority.
