## Inbox drafts and uncertain reply recovery — A03

[Inbox](/Users/shubham/Documents/Workspace/SocialEntangler/app/frontend/src/outreach/pages/OutreachInbox.js) keys drafts by workspace, sender, channel, thread and LinkedIn recipient/conversation identities. Each edit advances a local revision. Delayed fetches, AI suggestions and reply completions apply only to the captured selection and draft revision. An earlier send cannot erase newer text or transfer it to another recipient.

A reply UUID is saved before transport in tab-session storage with references only, never draft text or credentials. If storage is unavailable, sending is blocked. Remounting offers an explicit status check and never automatically resends. Pending/unknown outcomes retain the draft and prevent a blind second submission. The [server reply route](/Users/shubham/Documents/Workspace/SocialEntangler/app/outreach/api/inbox.py) binds the actor, workspace, request UUID, original payload digest and recipient revision; an exact retry returns the original job, and a changed payload conflicts. A status GET inspects that job without creating a new send. Original received evidence may complete projection repair without replay.

This is tab-session recovery, not durable cross-browser draft storage or guaranteed recovery after storage removal. Existing unresolved server slots remain held. Exact approval, paid access, freshness and execution guards remain required; these tests send no real replies.

## Truthful cancellation, integration lists and visible account state — A07/A10/A16/A17

- Crawl Stop checks both the cancellation response and a fresh terminal job state. A denied, conflicting, failed or unknown response keeps monitoring/recovery available. Stored job references permit explicit recovery after reopening. Server cancellation compares the original lease/status and reports an actual terminal state or conflict after a lost conditional write.
- API-key and webhook lists use strict workspace queries and explicit public-field allowlists. Real MongoDB ObjectIds serialize safely; credentials and future unexpected fields do not enter the response.
- Billing cancellation matches the captured invoice, term, paid metadata and field presence. It acknowledges a matched cancellation or an already-cancelled identical term. A renewed/replaced term conflicts; persistence failure remains retryable. Original cancellation timestamps survive retries. This does not claim provider subscription cancellation or release assigned IPs.
- Header, workspace and billing loads distinguish loading, unknown, failure, explicit trial and known paid state. Failed billing reads expose a retry and retain previously known entitlement rather than fabricate unpaid status. Risky cancellation/access-request controls are disabled while billing state is unknown. Curated prompt usage was already corrected in v2.56.

## Ordered common admission and warm-up cohorts — A04/A05/A06

[Common lead admission](/Users/shubham/Documents/Workspace/SocialEntangler/app/outreach/core/lead_importer.py) now runs campaign-state validation, suppression/deduplication, insertion or staged promotion, campaign counters and its event under the existing snapshot/majority workspace guard transaction. The transaction rereads the strict current campaign and writes its admission revision. Missing/deleted, warming, completed and retained activation-claim states deny admission. An active campaign linked to an Engage warm-up list requires pausing before intake.

Active admission verifies the selected current senders and sequence/version/revision, then supplies sender, root node, immutable version, enrollment UUID and campaign authorization revision. Draft/paused admissions remain explicitly unbound until launch. Mandatory DNC, withdrawn and email suppression apply to every source. Staged rows retain their original identity and import metadata when accepted; staged/unselected rows do not enter launch, warm-up, scheduling or enrolled counts.

Sheets, reviewed search, CSV/URL and public intake share that contract. A request accepts at most 1,000 rows; oversized requests are rejected rather than silently truncated. Reviewed search imports split explicit selected IDs into batches of 1,000, including older larger search jobs. Each batch has fresh admission, accepted rows are excluded from a retry, and partial/uncertain responses are visible. Closing/reopening the importer or changing campaign invalidates the old batch generation before it can issue another request. Counts returned by admission describe that request; older crawl-level DNC/duplicate statistics still describe the crawl job.

Public intake stores a workspace/key/body-bound idempotency receipt in `outreach_lead_admissions`; an exact retry returns its original result and a changed body conflicts. Current API-key scope/expiry and pilot policy are rechecked at final admission, with the key written in the transaction. The configuration flag and clock are process observations, not a cross-process atomic policy service. Account erasure includes these metadata receipts. Publication follows commit and carries only job/run references.

Warm-up/list settings, contact addition/removal and list deletion share the guard with admission and activation. Both old and newly linked campaign states are checked; retained claims and active/warming cohorts reject edits. Parent revisions fence captured activation decisions. Warm-up cohorts above 10,000 reject explicitly. This does not backfill older unbound enrollments or prove that arbitrary older/raw writers participate in the protocol.

## Resumable campaign deletion and conservative restore — A08

[Campaign lifecycle](/Users/shubham/Documents/Workspace/SocialEntangler/app/outreach/core/campaign_deletion.py) first stops the exact parent and commits a durable workspace/campaign/request journal in `outreach_campaign_deletions`. It increments authorization revision, pauses the campaign, disables auto-launch and retains a restore-review latch. Subsequent transactions process at most 200 child rows per step, with at most 20 steps per request. Every step writes the original Mongo parent identity and operation token before cleanup; a same-ID replacement cannot inherit the old operation.

Deletion cancels queued/pending/scheduled tasks, unassigns leads while recording their original stage, and marks matching sequences deleted. In-flight/uncertain evidence stays held. Incomplete operations return a pending status; the same UUID resumes committed progress after failure or lost acknowledgement. The UI saves workspace/campaign/request/phase references before transport and offers explicit Finish deletion, Finish restoration and Undo actions after remount. It never automatically resumes an operation. Expected workspace binding rejects an old view submitted under a different workspace.

Restore remains hidden until child restoration finishes. Only matching operation-marked leads still unassigned at the original unassigned stage are reattached; replacement assignments/stages survive. Sequences require the same marker. **Cancelled tasks stay cancelled, the campaign stays paused, and restore grants no execution authority.** Reply/suppression and enrollment identities are preserved, and enrolled counts are recomputed excluding staged rows. Old request UUIDs cannot delete a restored generation or restore a later deletion.

Legacy no-key retries can recover the current stopped operation, but cannot distinguish an old request across a completed restore. Older unjournaled partial deletions can clean up surviving links; missing/unmarked detached history is not reconstructed or reauthorized. Real transaction fault and simulated commit-then-response-loss checks are included; no SIGKILL, driver-unknown-commit or physical backup claim is added. Account erasure purges the ordinary lifecycle journal.

## Late Engage result fencing — A09

[Projection admission](/Users/shubham/Documents/Workspace/SocialEntangler/app/outreach/core/engage_projection.py) persists a separate fetch/enrichment run UUID before publishing reference-only work. Workers claim the queued run and capture the original list Mongo identity. Before provider reads and after provider awaits, the shared guard transaction writes the exact list/run/status and contact Mongo identity, profile URL, vanity and profile-URN presence/value. Empty, null and missing URNs remain distinct bindings.

Deleted/replaced lists or contacts, changed identities and superseded runs stop late projections. Posts, contact metadata and terminal counts/status are projected only under matching current bindings. Errors after claim finish only that captured run; an enrichment error does not overwrite fetch error state. Legacy three-argument broker messages stop before a provider call and require an explicit fresh fetch; they cannot guess the current generation.

Manual draft creation/update also fences the surviving list/contact/post in the transaction. New drafts record source generations; replacing a source makes an update conflict. Older drafts without these generation fields retain compatibility with surviving-parent checks, which does not authenticate historical lineage. Follow-up accuracy note: final v2.59 review found that the original v2.58 equality-to-null query could also match a removed field. The v2.59 repair adds explicit field existence and four native presence-change checks; the exact null-to-missing guarantee belongs to that follow-up. Existing orphan data is not automatically inventoried or erased here. Already-authorized HTTP cannot be recalled, and abrupt process death may still leave a run marked running; no new lease/crash recovery proof is claimed.
